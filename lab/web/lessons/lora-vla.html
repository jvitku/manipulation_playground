The question

Lessons 16–19 trained VLAs of a few million parameters from scratch. Real VLAs are pretrained at hundreds of millions to billions of parameters, and adapting one to a new robot means fine-tuning it — on a 10 GB consumer GPU, where training every weight of a 450 M-parameter SmolVLA does not even fit. This lesson asks whether LoRA — a frozen pretrained network plus low-rank adapters, about 1 % of the weights, written from scratch in src/nfv/lora.py — can match the standard fine-tune on the course's peg-in-hole demos, what it saves in memory and disk, and whether what full fine-tuning actually changes is low-rank at all.
