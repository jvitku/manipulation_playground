Sorting bolts and nuts with a force-aware VLA

A Panda with a Franka Hand picks realistic M16 bolts and nuts out of a bin, drops the bolts into a clearance hole and the nuts into bucket A. The robot senses torque at all seven joints and has simple tactile pads on its fingertips. Phase 1 builds the task and trains TD3, behaviour cloning and a fine-tuned SmolVLA without force. Phase 2 adds joint-torque and tactile sensing to all three, with a TA-VLA-style torque token for the VLA, aimed at better grasps and reliable insertion detection.

M16 · ISOISO 4017 bolts, ISO 4032 nuts, 17 mm clearance hole (ISO 273 fine)
7 + 32joint torque channels + tactile taxels (2 pads × 4 × 4)
3 learnersTD3 (skills), behaviour cloning (ACT), SmolVLA fine-tune
11 milestonesV0–V10, each gated by tests and a measured bar
01

Decisions already made

Answered on 2026-09-29. They fix the order of work and what "done" means in phase 1.

Demonstrations

Scripted stand-in first. A privileged scripted expert with a human-like noise model generates the demos, labelled source: synthetic in every episode. A human teleop recorder can come later; robosuite's keyboard and SpaceMouse drivers need an X display, so they would run on the host, not in the container.

VLA model

SmolVLA, fine-tuned locally on the RTX 3080 (10 GB). It has 450M parameters in total and a ~100M flow-matching action expert, predicts 50-step action chunks, and keeps its vision encoder frozen by default. The TA-VLA "smaller variant" means adding a torque token to SmolVLA's action expert.

Scene scope

Few parts first: 3 bolts and 3 nuts in the bin, one hole, and bucket A. The geometry, masses and friction are realistic from day one. Growing to a cluttered bucket is a later milestone once the pipeline works.

Dependencies

A separate VLA Docker image. LeRobot needs Python ≥ 3.12, torch ≥ 2.7 and numpy ≥ 2. The sim image stays on robosuite 1.5.2, MuJoCo 3.3.0, Python 3.11 and numpy < 2. The two images talk over TCP (see §6).

  1. A Franka Hand (80 mm stroke, 70 N continuous) grasps loose M16 bolts and nuts out of a bin and can slip or drop them.

Robot and sensor design

The design stays as close as possible to what a real Panda with a Franka Hand provides, so the lessons transfer. Anything that differs from robosuite's defaults is listed together with the reason.

Language

Episode instruction: "sort the parts: bolts into the hole, nuts into bucket A".

Per-part sub-instructions ("put the bolt in the hole", "put the nut in bucket A") are also stored, for a later hierarchical variant.

Why taxels are separate geoms. MuJoCo produces only a handful of contact points between two boxes, so a touch sensor laid over a single pad would give a blocky, mostly empty map. Making every taxel its own small collision geom, each with its own touch sensor, gives each taxel its own contacts and a true per-taxel force. Coarse, but honest for a "simple tactile sensor".

Success: a bolt
  • Shank ≥ 25 mm below the fixture's top face, and the axis tilted less than 5° from vertical.
  • No gripper contact (released), held for 1.0 s.
  • Then the bolt vanishes: its free joint is moved to a parking pose far below the table with zero velocity. MuJoCo cannot delete bodies at runtime.
Success: an episode
  • Every part sorted, with bolts vanished and nuts in bucket A, within 180 s of sim time (3,600 control steps).
  • Failure outcomes are logged separately: dropped outside, nut in the hole area, bolt jammed, force abort (|F̂ext| > 80 N) and timeout.
05

Task state machine

The scripted expert implements this loop with privileged state. The learned policies have to reproduce it from cameras, proprioception and, in phase 2, torque and tactile. Gold edges are decisions where force and touch should help.

flowchart TD
  start(["Episode start: 3 bolts + 3 nuts settled in the bin"]) --> select[Select next part]
  select --> approach[Approach and pre-grasp]
  approach --> grasp[Close gripper]
  grasp --> gok{"Grasp OK?"}
  gok -->|no| regrasp[Open and re-approach]
  regrasp --> approach
  gok -->|yes| lift[Lift and identify part]
  lift --> slip{"Slip?"}
  slip -->|dropped| select
  slip -->|held| kind{Bolt or nut?}
  kind -->|nut| bucket[Carry to bucket A and release]
  kind -->|bolt| reorient[Reorient shank-down]
  reorient --> tohole[Carry above the hole]
  tohole --> insert[Align, descend, spiral search]
  insert --> jam{"Jam?"}
  jam -->|yes| retract[Retract 3 mm and shift]
  retract --> insert
  jam -->|no| seated{"Seated?"}
  seated -->|no| insert
  seated -->|yes| release[Release, wait 1 s, bolt vanishes]
  bucket --> more{Parts left?}
  release --> more
  more -->|yes| select
  more -->|no| done(["Episode success"])
  class gok,slip,jam,seated sense
  classDef sense fill:#b8871f22,stroke:#b8871f,stroke-width:2px

Gold decisions and their sensing: Grasp OK? both pads in contact, normal force above 5 N · Slip? pad force lost or CoP drifting · Jam? joint-torque spike with no depth progress · Seated? depth, tilt and torque signature. Phase 2's claims are all about these four decisions. Better grasp means fewer regrasps and drops (tactile force and CoP). Better insertion detection means the seated test is answered from the torque signature and the tactile release rather than from privileged depth. Jam recovery uses joint torque the way Stage 1 used wrist torque.

The link is Python's standard-library multiprocessing.connection (TCP plus pickle with an auth key), so it needs no new dependency. The client sends images, state, torque and tactile history and the instruction; the server returns a 50-step action chunk and the client executes the first n. TD3 trains directly against the env in the existing train image.

07

The learners, phase 1 and phase 2

TD3 · skills, not the whole sort

Long-horizon RL from pixels is out of reach at this budget, so TD3 learns the two contact-rich skills on state, reusing the Stage 1.5 recipe (100 demos plus TD3+BC with a BC floor). A scripted sequencer chains them.

  • Insert: bolt in hand, with an unknown in-hand offset and tilt (± 3 mm, ± 5°) observable only through tactile CoP and joint torque.
  • Pick: grasp one given part from the bin, with success measured after lift and shake.
  • Phase 2: add the τext history and the tactile summary to the observation; compare with and without, 10 seeds each.
Behaviour cloning · ACT

LeRobot's ACT (ResNet-18 image encoder plus a transformer, action chunks) trained from scratch on the demos. It is the standard BC baseline for the VLA.

  • Inputs: 2 cameras + proprio (+ instruction id).
  • Phase 2: a force token (τext history and tactile summary → MLP → one token), the design that worked in Stage 1. Dropout 0 on that path: Stage 1.5 R5 showed dropout erases rare, sharp contact cues.
SmolVLA → TA-SmolVLA

Fine-tune lerobot/smolvla_base with a frozen VLM, training the action expert and the state projection (the SmolVLA defaults).

  • Phase 2, per TA-VLA: the torque history (10 frames over 2 s) is flattened into one token and fed to the action expert (decoder), not the VLM prefix.
  • Joint loss L = L_action + β·L_torque with β = 0.1: the expert also predicts the future torque chunk.
  • SmolVLA has no suffix state slot (state lives in the prefix), so the token is prepended to the suffix in our own subclass.

TA-SmolVLA: where the torque token goes

Gold nodes are new. The 15 channels per frame are τext (7) and the tactile summary per pad: normal force, CoP x, CoP y and shear (2 × 4 = 8). The ablation "torque token zeroed" is built into the same model, so the comparison changes only the information, not the architecture.

Key modules

Patterns only. Each snippet shows the core decision in the module, not the full implementation.

src/fvb/sensors/joint_torque.py · measured vs external torque
def read(self) -> tuple[np.ndarray, np.ndarray]:
    d, m = self.d, self.m
    # link-side torque: actuator + passive (damping, friction)
    tau_J = d.sensordata[self.adr] + d.qfrc_passive[self.dofs]
    # model torque for the current (q, qd, qdd)
    self.scratch.qpos[:] = d.qpos; self.scratch.qvel[:] = d.qvel
    self.scratch.qacc[:] = d.qacc
    mujoco.mj_inverse(m, self.scratch)
    tau_model = self.scratch.qfrc_inverse[self.dofs]
    tau_ext = tau_J - tau_model            # ~ Franka tau_ext_hat
    n = self.rng.normal(0, 1, 7) * self.noise_std
    return tau_J + n + self.bias, tau_ext + n + self.bias
src/fvb/sensors/tactile.py · taxel grid on each pad
def add_tactile_pads(gripper_xml, rows=4, cols=4, pitch=0.004):
    """Replace each finger's pad box by rows x cols taxel geoms,
    each with its own touch sensor; one force sensor per pad."""
    for side in ("finger1", "finger2"):
        pad = find_geom(gripper_xml, f"{side}_pad_collision")
        for i, j in np.ndindex(rows, cols):
            g = taxel_geom(pad, i, j, pitch, friction=1.0)
            site = taxel_site(g)                # slightly larger
            add_sensor("touch", f"{side}_tx{i}{j}", site)
        add_sensor("force", f"{side}_padF", pad_site(pad))
src/fvb/envs/sort_bolts_nuts.py · seat, dwell, vanish
def _update_parts(self, t: float) -> None:
    for p in self.parts:
        if p.kind == "bolt" and p.status != "vanished":
            ok = (self._shank_depth(p) >= P.seat_depth
                  and self._tilt_deg(p) < 5.0
                  and not self._touching_gripper(p))
            if not ok:
                p.seated_since = None
            elif p.seated_since is None:
                p.seated_since = t
            elif t - p.seated_since >= P.vanish_after:
                self._park(p)                  # free joint far away
                p.status = "vanished"
        elif p.kind == "nut" and self._in_bucket_at_rest(p):
            p.status = "in_bucket"
src/fvb/vla/ta_smolvla.py · decoder-side torque token
class TASmolVLA(VLAFlowMatching):   # lerobot's model, subclassed
    def embed_suffix(self, noisy_actions, timestep, torque_hist):
        embs, pad, att = super().embed_suffix(noisy_actions, timestep)
        tok = self.torque_mlp(torque_hist.flatten(1))[:, None]  # (B,1,W)
        embs = torch.cat([tok, embs], dim=1)          # prepend
        pad = torch.cat([pad[:, :1], pad], dim=1)
        att = torch.cat([att[:, :1], att], dim=1)
        return embs, pad, att

    def loss(self, v_pred, u_t, tq_pred, tq_target):
        return mse(v_pred, u_t) + 0.1 * mse(tq_pred, tq_target)
Verify in V0. The embed_suffix signature and the attention-mask conventions come from the current LeRobot source; they change between releases. V0 pins one LeRobot version and a unit test checks that TA-SmolVLA with a zeroed torque token produces the same action shapes and masks as SmolVLA.

Edge cases

ScenarioExpected behaviour
Bolt standing on its head in the binExpert grasps the head side-on; RL/BC may knock it over. Spawns sample poses by dropping, so this occurs naturally.
Two parts grasped at onceDetected (two part bodies in pad contact); counted as a grasp failure; the expert opens and retries.
Bolt jammed tilted in the chamferNot seated (tilt ≥ 5° or depth < 25 mm); the joint-torque jam signature triggers a retract. Never counted as a success.
Bolt seated but still grippedThe dwell timer starts only after release (no pad contact), so a grasped bolt never vanishes.
Nut on the bucket rimNot counted until below the rim and at rest; a later fall inside counts.
Nut dropped into the fixture areaLogged as nut in the hole area; may block the hole, which the policy must clear or the episode times out.
Part leaves the tableStatus dropped; the episode can still succeed only if every remaining part is sorted (otherwise a partial score).
Spawn overlapRejection sampling at spawn, then 1 s of settling; seeds whose settle fails are skipped deterministically.
Thin nut walls tunnelling1 ms physics step (Stage 0 M3), convex ring pieces ≥ 4 mm thick; V1 drop test checks penetration.
Force abort|F̂ext| > 80 N ends the episode (logged), as the 60 N abort did for Stage 1.
Policy server slow or downThe client times out (1 s) and marks the episode infra error, not a policy failure; nothing is silently skipped.
Normalisation mismatch sim ↔ serverDataset stats ship with the checkpoint; the client sends raw values and the server normalises; a round-trip test checks an expert episode re-encodes exactly.
GPU OOM in fine-tuningV0 measures the limit; the config uses the frozen VLM, bf16 and gradient accumulation; the batch is recorded in the checkpoint.
Reward hackingSuccess is defined geometrically (depth, tilt, released, dwell, in-bucket at rest). Stage 1.5's "lowered beside the block" lesson becomes a regression test from day one.

Implementation notes and risks

Backward compatibility. Nothing in Stage 0–1.5 changes behaviour: new envs register alongside PegInHole; the §5 schema only gains optional keys; the sim and train images keep their pins; all 28 existing tests stay green at every milestone.
Critical.
  • Version wall. LeRobot (Python ≥ 3.12, numpy ≥ 2) cannot share an environment with robosuite 1.5.2 (numpy < 2). The server/client split is not optional.
  • SmolVLA's state lives in the VLM prefix. TA-VLA's finding is that torque belongs in the decoder, so the torque token goes into the action expert's suffix through our subclass, pinned to one LeRobot release.
  • Synthetic ≠ human. Every scripted demo carries source: synthetic, and results say so. Human demos need a teleop recorder on the host (X display for robosuite's keyboard/SpaceMouse drivers), a later milestone.
  • Reorienting a bolt lying flat is the hardest motion for the expert (pitching the wrist ~90° while holding the shank). V4 may need a regrasp or reorientation step; this is the main schedule risk in phase 1.
Performance.
  • Two 256² cameras at 20 Hz need EGL on the GPU; OSMesa on the CPU would dominate episode time.
  • SmolVLA inference is a VLM prefix pass plus 10 flow steps per chunk. Executing several steps of each 50-step chunk keeps the closed loop near real time; V0 measures the latency.
  • RL stays on state (skills), not pixels; Stage 1.5 showed even state-based TD3 needs demos and a BC floor to be reliable.
Assumptions to verify early. The joint-torque noise σ and bias (set from the Franka spec in V2); whether MuJoCo's touch sensors resolve 4 mm taxels cleanly (V2 test); the ≥ 90 % expert rate (V4); SmolVLA fitting in 10 GB with frozen VLM and bf16 (V0).
